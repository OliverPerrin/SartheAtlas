# Hypercar and Oreca exterior asset research

Checked live on 2026-09-30. Public Sketchfab metadata and public thumbnail images only; no protected viewer assets, meshes, download archives, game files, accounts or purchases were accessed. No renderer or reference code changed by this research task.

## Recommended shortlist

Toyota TR010, Genesis GMR001 and Peugeot 9X8 EVO have materially better, single-car textured candidates than the earlier catalogue. Ferrari Project212, Cadillac, Alpine and current Oreca remain reasonable freely viewable exterior references. BMW has a credited source upload that is preferable to its vecarz repost.

Aston remains an unresolved exact-LMH realism gap. The race-named MattDoesBlender models are untextured white previews; the textured vecarz model names the non-homologated customer LM variant and discloses third-party sourcing. It can be an explicitly labelled variant reference, but it cannot establish an accurate WEC LMH model.

Visual judgements below come from inspecting downloaded public thumbnails, including full 1920x1080 Ferrari/BMW/Cadillac/Alpine images. Thumbnail quality does not prove interactive viewer readiness or exact homologation geometry. The T3 browser host was unavailable to root during this pass, so no new full-viewer readiness claim is made.

## Variant evidence from manufacturers

- Toyota confirms 2026 TR010 has revised front and rear bodywork, replacing the earlier GR010 identity: [official team specification](https://toyota-racing.com/toyota-racing-wec/), [2026 brand/model announcement](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/toyota/43839258.html). The LMP12 thumbnail visibly includes wider horizontal headlamps and the red/white 2026 appearance; that is a thumbnail-level resemblance, not full geometric validation.
- Aston describes Valkyrie LM as a non-homologated customer car derived from the race Hypercar: [official LM announcement](https://www.astonmartin.com/en-us/our-world/news/2025/6/9/aston-martin-valkyrie-lm-offers-ultimate-le-mans-hypercar-track-experience). The production road Valkyrie and AMR Pro are also different variants; neither should substitute for a verified race LMH.
- Peugeot original no-wing variant is stated by its uploader: [existing model page](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/peugeot-9x8-hypercar-f77715deedb14cda8afe96a83c4daf31). The replacement is explicitly labelled [2024 9X8 EVO](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2024-peugeot-9x8-evo-b3efbf3a774c4411983e630d764c8cea).

### Aston public interactive viewers, confirmed live

The official Sketchfab Viewer API reached `viewerready` for both `ed9902ea674642d38cc1738874be8156` and `7daa766d301f4d489312b0922d4bdc0d`. Only documented API material names/channel factors and node names/material associations were inspected. No vertex data, meshes, texture files or protected viewer endpoints were accessed.

- **Textured LM-labelled vecarz model**: 61 materials; body (`EXT_CARPAINT`), decals, tyres, glass, carbon and cockpit have colour maps. It is materially more realistic than the plain-white LMH upload. Its LM versus race-LMH identity remains unverified; the title alone should not be mistaken for homologation evidence. Repost disclaimer remains relevant. Viewer screenshot: `/Users/oliverperrin/.t3/userdata/browser-artifacts/browser-screenshot-sketchfab-com-muo8vhzb-575f6506.png`.
- **MattDoesBlender LMH model**: 79 materials; inspected body, glass and tyre colour channels lack albedo maps. A temporary local-browser-only green/black material experiment via documented `setMaterial` succeeded for11 exact names. This improves its presentation but does not supply authentic textures, sponsor graphics or verified geometry. It is not the strongest default exterior. Recoloured screenshot: `/Users/oliverperrin/.t3/userdata/browser-artifacts/browser-screenshot-sketchfab-com-muo8vhu1-7454440e.png`. No website files or creator-hosted source were changed by the experiment.

### BMW credited source, confirmed live

`9871477854b64e82bd43438eb9b940fd` by JUSTGAME reached ViewerAPI ready with43 materials,42 of which have albedo maps. Its single-car silhouette, number25 logos and earlier BMW GTP bodywork visually match the existing vecarz reference. Existing vecarz metadata explicitly credits JUSTGAME; 432,150 versus432,126 triangles further supports a repost relationship, although binary mesh identity was not checked or claimed. Prefer the credited source upload with its earlier-specification caveat.

Source screenshot: `/Users/oliverperrin/.t3/userdata/browser-artifacts/browser-screenshot-sketchfab-com-muo9008f-1fc836a3.png`.

Exact source material names `Body` and `Body.001` have texture-mapped AlbedoPBR factor1, metalness0 and roughness.6; `EXT_Tyre` has the same scalar values.
