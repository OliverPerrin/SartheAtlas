# GT exterior asset research

Checked 30 September 2026. This is a sourcing report, not an implementation or a claim that all nine cars are now exact replicas. Only public Sketchfab model metadata, creator pages, published thumbnails and the normal hosted viewer were inspected. No model files were downloaded, purchased, converted, extracted from games or taken from viewer internals.

## Provenance and specification boundaries

- The [MattDoesBlender profile](https://sketchfab.com/MattDoesBlender) says some uploaded models may belong to others and can be removed on request. Several model names refer to games or unpacked files. This does not prove every upload is unauthorized, but ownership and upstream licenses remain unresolved for direct redistribution.
- The [vecarz Mustang listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ford-mustang-gt3-2024-wwwvecarzcom-edc0d377ada74c8d8dcf12f719f81c65) describes third-party Facebook, VK and MediaFire sourcing. Other vecarz entries credit MattDoesBlender or toddeppe. This is a visible upstream chain, not independent creator-rights evidence.
- The recommended Vantage and conditional Corvette by toddeppe explicitly credit MattDoesBlender. Credit both levels for hosted references; the original upstream model is unspecified on the Vantage page.
- [Aston Martin’s 2024 GT3 announcement](https://www.astonmartin.com/en/our-world/news/2024/2/12/state-of-the-art-aston-martin-vantage-gt3-spearheads-new-era-in-top-flight-gt-racing) confirms a revised body and broader front grille for the 2024 car. The older rounded-headlight models must not stand in for this current design.
- No road-going Mustang GTD, road Porsche GT3 RS, GT4 Vantage or GTE Corvette was silently substituted for a GT3 car. Ambiguous Corvette C8R naming stays explicit.

- Orbit through synthetic UI events was not confirmed, so the Vantage front has not been inspected from multiple angles here. Thumbnail and visible side-view identity are bounded evidence, not manufacturer-CAD accuracy.

- No inspected public page supplied a provenance-verified, anonymous direct download that can currently be treated as fully cleared for hosting all nine cars. A downloadable flag alone may still require a Sketchfab account, creator contact and upstream-rights checks.
- For a first-view upgrade, use the visually verified creator thumbnail/hosted viewer with honest source/year captions and loading fallback. Do not present the educational procedural chassis as the authentic exterior or relabel an earlier reference as the 2026 car.

## Research method

Monid 0.1.7 discovery was run for Sketchfab model search and returned no relevant retrieval endpoint. No paid Monid calls were made. Search then used public creator pages and the documented public Sketchfab `/v3/models/{uid}` and `/v3/search?type=models` endpoints. Temporary metadata and thumbnail inspection files are in `/tmp/sarthe-gt-model-research`; they are not bundled product assets.

## Corvette follow-up: named-material restoration verified

This follow-up supersedes the conditional Corvette recommendation above. Prefer the explicitly named Z06 GT3.R model `d76261866d3047d4b2f76993bf92f85e` with a disclosed plain studio finish. Do not present the painted `4f351ff...` asset as a confirmed title typo.

The official Sketchfab Viewer SDK was initialized on a local scratch page on 30 September 2026. Supported `getMaterialList`, `getSceneGraph`, `getCameraLookAt`, `getShadingStyle`, `setCameraLookAt`, `setMaterial` and `setEnvironment` calls were used. All changes were temporary viewer presentation changes. No mesh, UV, geometry or texture payload was extracted.

Observed source state for `d762...`:

- Public metadata: 134,846 vertices and 233,273 triangles.
- Scene root: `corvette_z06_gt3r.fbx`, with body, chassis, cockpit and wheel groups.
- 34 independently named materials: car paint, exterior/interior glass, carbon, grille, tyre wall/tread, static rims, rim blur, brakes, metal parts and cockpit details.
- Every AlbedoPBR material initially had the same `[0.8, 0.8, 0.8]` colour and no bound base-colour texture. The live model itself was white, not only its thumbnail.
- Material records contained an enabled Matcap channel, but the supported global shading query returned `pbr` / `lit`. Updating named PBR colours and finish factors visibly worked. The Matcap enable flag still appeared true when read back, so it must not be used alone to diagnose the active renderer.
- Base-colour, metalness, roughness and opacity channels could be changed through `setMaterial`; transparent glass and distinct rubber, painted body and metal finishes rendered correctly.
- The source also displays opaque `EXT_RIMS_BLUR` geometry over the static spokes. Setting that material's opacity to zero revealed the existing spoke geometry. This is a static showroom view, not a new wheel model.

The verified recipe is in `src/data/materialRecipes.ts`. It covers all 34 observed names, retaining source misspellings such as `EXT_EXAULST`, `EXT_MECANICALS_PARTS` and `INT_ROOLCAGE`. Body paint is plain yellow with a clear coat; rubber and carbon are dark; rims and brakes have metal finishes; glass uses transparency. Blank decal surfaces are hidden rather than inventing logos or entry markings. These are presentation choices, not manufacturer material measurements or a recreation of any team's livery.

The yellow restoration was inspected from front three-quarter and side views. Its large single hood outlet, Z06-derived triangular side intake, front race lighting and raised racing wing are consistent with the GT3.R family shown in [GM's reveal](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2023/jan/0127-corvette-racing.html) and [Corvette Racing's exterior reference](https://corvetteracing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Exterior-1.jpg). GM identifies the C8.R as the predecessor and specifically describes the GT3.R's changed hood extraction, production-derived side ducts and new wing. This establishes a stronger family match than a title alone; exact 2026 bodywork and manufacturer-CAD accuracy remain unverified.

The painted `4f351ff...` model is not identical to `d762...` based on the observed API evidence:

## Latest textured Corvette and Ford comparison

This update supersedes the earlier plain-yellow Corvette finish and untextured Ford recommendations. The final implementation should prefer creator-authored texture maps and disclose the model's own year/livery.

### Corvette: textured exact-family upload

[Kevin Love SketchFab's Corvette Z06 GT3R](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/corvette-z06-gt3r-4dedaf758f2d4f659322efffc969e8e3), UID `4dedaf758f2d4f659322efffc969e8e3`, was published 21 August 2026. Public metadata lists CC BY and 233,047 triangles. Its supported SDK scene graph contains `corvette_z06_gt3r.fbx_1`; 90 materials and 25 registered textures provide the detailed yellow/black #13 creator livery, cockpit, carbon, grille, wheels and lighting. This is a substantially stronger initial visual than recolouring the untextured `d762...` asset. The base family retains the single hood outlet and Z06 side-intake form checked against the official reference. Exact manufacturer surface accuracy and 2026 entry livery remain unverified.

The following exact motion-blur group names can safely be hidden through `getSceneGraph` and `hide`; the existing static wheel spokes remain visible:

```json
["RIM_BLUR_LF_134", "RIM_BLUR_LR_147", "RIM_BLUR_RF_160", "RIM_BLUR_RR_173"]
```

A remaining source defect is the red upper front lamps. Supported picking and rear-view verification established that both upper front lamps and rear tail lamps share the same Geometry node `600`, parent `EXT_LIGHTS_57`, material `EXT_LIGHTS_1`. That material's source base colour is red. Making the material white fixes the front but incorrectly whitens the rear; therefore no global white override for `EXT_LIGHTS_1` is recommended.

The front outer-lens material is exactly `EXT_LIGHTS_GLASS_FRONT`; its source colour aliases and opacity channel reference texture `08e432d2ced749a2bd9c9aad717d711d`. Testing `texture: null` on `AlbedoPBR`, `DiffusePBR` and `DiffuseColor` worked through the supported API. It did not eliminate red underlying lamps on its own. A fully opaque dark lens hides the wrong red but also covers the auxiliary LED lenses, because the front-lens Geometry combines both. Preserving or inverting its opacity texture did not provide a complete correction. Neither approach should be described as an exact restoration of the real lamp optics.

The white-inner-lamp experiment and opaque-lens experiment are rejected as final optics fixes. Body texture maps and rear lamp colours should remain untouched. Viewer-only projected decal experiments were not adopted; no decal geometry or screenshot was added to product assets.

The `a9bd82c632274806b64496416adbbfa2` alternative was also inspected live. Its explicit Z06 GT3.R object/material names are useful family evidence, but its 22 materials/15 textures have incomplete finish configuration and an orange custom skin. Hiding `CarShadowProjection_Plane_0`, `CarTrail_39` and `Chevrolet_Corvette_Z06_GT3_R_LOD1_65` removes stage extras and overlapping LOD geometry. Correcting finish factors improves it, but it remains weaker than Kevin's authored textured upload and is not selected.

### Ford: creator-restored reference preferred

The original Kevin Ford `d2b7823f48f147e99e857ee825d089cc` was confirmed to have 62 materials and **zero registered textures** in the live supported SDK. Its grey body/orange glass preview is an incomplete finish, not an adequate default for the realism objective.

MattDoesBlender's `545a596dde5f464ab166662dd48e57a0` is much better: 39 materials, 47 textures, a yellow GT3 racing livery, Goodyear tyre markings and a detailed transparent cockpit. Its authored static view still displays grey wheel-disc overlays. Generic mesh names make a safe exact wheel-only hide uncertain; no unverified node removal is recommended.

Prefer [vecarz's restored Mustang GT3](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ford-mustang-gt3-wwwvecarzcom-061487e4e4fa4cd1bf7e15eae2e238ba), UID `061487e4e4fa4cd1bf7e15eae2e238ba`. It was published 24 July 2026 and has 214,926 vertices, 375,545 triangles, 39 materials and 47 registered textures. The normal hosted viewer was verified with visible static spokes, distinct carbon/paint finishes, transparent windows and headlight detail. It depicts its own yellow #44 livery, not necessarily the selected catalog entry or 2026 Evo specification.

Uploader credit: [vecarz / heynic](https://sketchfab.com/heynic). The subsequent live SDK inspection of [Patricars' Chevrolet Corvette C8 Z06 GT3](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/chevrolet-corvette-c8-z06-gt3-2752ec60ebad4b7496dd3dc7005eca90), UID `2752ec60ebad4b7496dd3dc7005eca90`, resolves the earlier uncertainty about its scene identity. This is the preferred clean Corvette candidate: it has 217,319 triangles, 33 materials and 10 registered textures, with a professional plain-white racing exterior, existing clear headlight optics, detailed auxiliary LEDs, static wheel spokes and carbon trim. The live front three-quarter view was inspected in the normal hosted viewer; no runtime body recolour, new decal geometry or optical masking was needed. Exact manufacturer-CAD fidelity and 2026 bodywork remain unverified.
